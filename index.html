01 / ABOUT

The person behind Klightten

I learn best when an idea has to survive the real world.

I’m Jaru, a BSIT student at Pateros Technological College and an aspiring cloud engineer. When software, hardware, or Linux does not work the way I need, I study the problem and try to build a better answer.

That habit grew into the KLIGHTTEN ecosystem: experiments in desktop customization, hardware control, media, mobile apps, databases, and artificial intelligence. I care about useful design, understandable systems, and software that respects limited hardware.
